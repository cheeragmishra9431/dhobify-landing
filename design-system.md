# Dhobify Design System

Version: 1.0
Positioning: ⚡ Fast & Bold Urban Tech Brand

---

# 1. Brand Personality

Dhobify visual identity must feel:

* ⚡ Fast
* 🟡 Bold
* 🏙 Urban
* 📱 Tech-first
* 💪 Confident
* Clean but not soft
* Modern but not playful-childish

Avoid:

* Luxury softness
* Traditional laundry vibes
* Overuse of bright colors
* Too many visual elements

---

# 2. Color System

## 2.1 Primary Brand Colors

### 🟡 Primary Yellow (Energy)

HEX: `#FACC15`

Usage:

* Primary CTA buttons
* Important highlights
* Icons
* Underlines
* Key numbers
* Active states

Hover State:
HEX: `#EAB308`

Rules:

* Never use as full background section
* Never use for paragraph text
* Use intentionally for emphasis only

---

### ⚫ Primary Black (Authority)

HEX: `#111111`

Usage:

* Headings
* Navbar
* Footer
* Strong emphasis text

---

### ⚫ Deep Black (Dark Section Background)

HEX: `#0F0F0F`

Usage:

* Dark CTA sections
* Footer background
* High-contrast sections

---

## 2.2 Supporting Neutrals

### ⚪ White

HEX: `#FFFFFF`

Primary background color.

---

### ⚪ Light Grey

HEX: `#F5F5F5`

Usage:

* Alternate section backgrounds
* Cards background
* Subtle separation between sections

---

### ⚫ Body Text Grey

HEX: `#4B5563`

Usage:

* Paragraph text
* Descriptions
* Secondary information

---

# 3. Color Hierarchy Rules

Visual Priority Order:

1. Yellow (attention)
2. Black (authority)
3. White (space)
4. Grey (supporting)

Never introduce new brand colors without approval.

No gradients.
No additional accent colors.
No red/orange in UI (logo only).

---

# 4. Typography System

## 4.1 Font Stack

Headings:

* Poppins ExtraBold (preferred)
* Fallback: Montserrat Bold

Body:

* Inter Regular / Medium

---

## 4.2 Typography Scale

Hero Heading:

* 48px–64px desktop
* 32px mobile
* Font-weight: 800+

Section Heading:

* 32px desktop
* 24px mobile
* Font-weight: 700–800

Body Text:

* 16px–18px
* Line-height: 1.6
* Color: #4B5563

Small Text:

* 14px
* Muted grey

---

# 5. Button System

## 5.1 Primary Button

Background: `#FACC15`
Text: `#111111`
Border Radius: 8px
Padding: Generous (12–16px vertical)

Hover:
Background: `#EAB308`

Shadow:
Subtle shadow on hover only.

Usage:
Download App (primary CTA)

---

## 5.2 Secondary Button

Style: Outline

Border: 2px solid `#111111`
Text: `#111111`
Background: Transparent

Hover:
Background: `#111111`
Text: `#FFFFFF`

Usage:
Become Partner
Request Investor Deck

---

# 6. Layout System

## 6.1 Spacing

Section Padding:

* 80px–100px desktop
* 60px mobile

Content Max Width:

* 1200px

Grid:

* 12-column layout (desktop)
* Single column (mobile)

White space is critical.
Avoid clutter.

---

# 7. Section Styling Rules

Hero:

* White background
* Bold typography
* High contrast

Alternate sections:

* Use #F5F5F5 background

Dark CTA section:

* #0F0F0F background
* Yellow CTA button

Never:

* Stack too many dark sections together
* Use yellow backgrounds for full sections

---

# 8. Iconography

Style:

* Simple
* Line or minimal filled
* Black or yellow only

Avoid:

* Multi-color icons
* Complex illustrations
* Cartoon style

---

# 9. Card Design

Card Background:
#FFFFFF

Border:
1px subtle grey OR none

Shadow:
Very soft shadow

Border Radius:
12px max

Keep clean and sharp.

---

# 10. Animation Guidelines

Allowed:

* Subtle fade-in
* Slight upward motion
* Button hover micro-interaction

Not Allowed:

* Bounce effects
* Over-animated sections
* Distracting transitions

Fast brand ≠ flashy brand.

---

# 11. Accessibility Rules

* Minimum contrast ratio 4.5:1
* Yellow text never on white
* Buttons clearly distinguishable
* Tap targets minimum 44px height

---

# 12. Logo Usage Rules

* Maintain clear space around logo
* Do not stretch or distort
* Use original logo colors
* Avoid placing logo on yellow background

---

# 13. Future App Consistency

This system must be used across:

* Landing page
* Web dashboard
* Customer app
* Partner app
* Marketing materials

No visual drift allowed.
