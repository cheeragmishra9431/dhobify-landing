# Dhobify Landing Page Scope

## 1. Purpose

The Dhobify landing page is designed to achieve three primary objectives:

1. Drive customer app downloads (primary goal)
2. Attract laundry partners
3. Establish credibility for potential investors

This page is marketing-focused and does not include backend logic or dynamic integrations at this stage.

---

## 2. Brand Positioning

Dhobify is positioned as:

* ⚡ Fast
* 🟡 Bold
* 🏙 Urban
* 📱 Tech-first
* 💪 Confident

Tone: Bold, confident, slightly playful but still trustworthy.

Not luxury. Not soft. Not traditional laundry branding.

---

## 3. Brand Colors (Finalized)

Primary Yellow: #FACC15
Yellow Hover: #EAB308
Primary Black: #111111
Deep Black: #0F0F0F
White: #FFFFFF
Light Grey: #F5F5F5
Body Grey: #4B5563

Strict Rules:

* No red in UI (only inside logo if required)
* No orange in UI
* No gradients
* No full yellow background sections
* Yellow is used primarily for CTAs and highlights

---

## 4. Typography

Headings:

* Poppins ExtraBold (preferred)
* Fallback: Montserrat Bold

Body:

* Inter
* Clean, readable, modern

Design style:

* Heavy headline typography
* Generous white space
* Mobile-first layout

---

## 5. Primary Conversion Goal

Primary CTA:
📲 Download App

This must:

* Appear above the fold
* Be visually dominant
* Appear at least 3 times on the page
* Be highly visible on mobile

Secondary CTAs:

* Become a Partner
* Request Investor Deck

Primary CTA always has visual priority.

---

## 6. Section Structure (Final Order)

1. Navbar
2. Hero Section
3. Problem → Solution Section
4. How Dhobify Works (3 Steps)
5. Services Section
6. Why Dhobify
7. Partner Section
8. Investor / Vision Section
9. FAQ Section
10. Final CTA Section
11. Footer

No additional sections without explicit decision.

## 7. Section Details

### Hero Section

Headline:
“Why Stick to One Laundry Store?”

“Tired of Random Laundry Stores? Compare, Choose & Get It Delivered.”

Must include:

* Primary CTA (Download App)
* Secondary CTAs (Partner, Investor)
* Serving HSR Layout mention
* App mockup on right (desktop view)

### Problem Section

4 pain points displayed in card layout:

* No time to wash & iron
* No pickup options
* No price transparency
* No tracking

Dhobify connects you to verified laundry partners with doorstep pickup.

1. Choose store & book pickup
2. We collect from your doorstep
3. Fresh clothes delivered in 24–48 hours

Simple and visual.

* Wash & Iron

* Steam Iron

Free pickup on orders above ₹300

## 8. Technical Requirements

* React-based implementation
* Component-based architecture
* Mobile-first responsive design
* No inline styles
* Reusable Button component
* Reusable Section wrapper component
* Clean folder structure
* Optimized for performance

No backend integration required in MVP landing page.

---

## 9. Performance & UX Rules

* Above-the-fold clarity
* Fast loading
* Clear spacing between sections
* No clutter
* Strong contrast
* CTA visible within 3 seconds
* Clean animations only if subtle

---

## 10. Future Integration (Not in Scope Now)

* App store links
* API integrations
* Analytics
* Form backend submission
* CMS integration

These are Phase 2 items.
