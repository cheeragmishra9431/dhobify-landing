Delete or update some personal data (keep your account)

This page explains how you can remove or change certain personal data in the Dhobify mobile app while keeping your sign-in and account. On Google Play, the app is listed as Dhobify. It does not replace full account deletion.

In the app: update or remove your own data

Use the Dhobify app on your device. The steps below are self-service only (no email or call required for these items):

  1. Open the Dhobify app.
  2. Saved addresses: Go to Profile, then Addresses. From there you can add, edit, or remove saved delivery addresses.
  3. Name on your account: Go to Profile or Account (whichever label your app version shows) and update the name field—for example, shorten it or change how you want to be identified in the app.
  4. Order you are building: If you are on a screen where you choose services or items for a new order, clear any selections you no longer want before you confirm the order, so nothing unintended stays queued for checkout.
  5. Notifications (if your version shows them): If Profile or Settings includes options for push or marketing notifications, adjust those toggles there.

Your registered phone number is used to sign in with OTP. Changing or removing it is not covered by the simple profile edits above; contact support if you need help with your phone number on file.

Delete entire account

To permanently delete your account and the data removed by our account deletion process, follow the in-app flow described here:

https://www.dhobify.in/account-deletion

What may still be kept

Even after you update or remove data in the app, some information can remain for legal, tax, and operational reasons—for example records linked to orders you already placed (including amounts and status), internal logs, and database backups. Dhobify is primarily a cash-on-delivery service; we do not rely on stored payment cards in the app the way a card-on-file business would, but commercial and audit records may still exist as described in our policies.

For a fuller explanation of account deletion, backups, and retention, see Account deletion and our Privacy Policy (https://www.dhobify.in/privacy).
